How a pilot runs

  • BeforeWe load your guest file, your cars and your programme with your team, in the weeks before the event.
  • On the daySomeone from our team is on site for the first day, and we check in with you every day of the event.
  • AfterWe compare the event with how you work today, on measures agreed before it starts.

What we measure

The measures are chosen with you on day one. For example: the hours your team spends on coordination, the messages and calls it takes, the problems caught before a guest noticed them, and how the team felt at the end of each day.

Designed with the agencies who run the events

Ellie is shaped on the ground with its first agency. These are the parts we are designing together now.

  • The driver app, with live position switched on by the driver
  • Delays and changes on the day
  • The review after the event, with costs by guest and by tier
  • Preferences kept from one event to the next
  • Incident protocols for the whole team
